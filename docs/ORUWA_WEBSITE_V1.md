# ORUWA Website v1

## Positioning

ORUWA is a Business Operations Platform for Japanese small businesses. ORUWA Cafe is the first real vertical product. The website sells reduced operational friction, not a speculative feature list.

## Sitemap

Japanese: `/`, `/products`, `/cafe`, `/pricing`, `/security`, `/about`, `/contact`, `/login`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/commercial-disclosure`.

English: `/en`, `/en/products`, `/en/cafe`, `/en/pricing`, `/en/security`, `/en/about`, `/en/contact`, `/en/login`.

The homepage is platform-first. `/products` is generated from a centralized package registry. ORUWA Cafe is the first completed package, not the full definition of ORUWA. Additional packages can be registered when their scope and availability are confirmed.

## Design system

Warm ivory surfaces, ink/navy management color, ORUWA green for normal/success state, amber for attention and muted red for shortage. Typography uses Geist with Japanese system fallbacks. Product visuals are neutral UI compositions, not customer screenshots.

Temporary logo direction: replaceable typographic `ORUWA` wordmark with a compact letterform mark. Future options: an operational loop, linked modules, or a restrained Japanese seal-inspired geometry. No trademark symbol is used.

## Product truth

- No customer count, testimonial, rating, fixed savings or guaranteed launch time.
- IZUMI IT COMPANY experience is explicitly distinguished from ORUWA adoption.
- Pricing remains `inquiry`; no amount renders until approved centrally.
- Machine translation is described with original-text verification.
- Future products, Customer Portal and admin tools are not presented as available.
- Contact submission is disabled rather than showing false success.
- Legal pages are marked templates pending owner/professional confirmation.

## Boundaries and future connections

`oruwa.jp` is marketing only. Future `account.oruwa.jp`, `app.oruwa.jp` and internal admin remain separate applications. `accountUrl`, `appUrl`, `statusUrl` and `supportUrl` remain empty until approved. Demo, consultation, pricing and developer links live in `lib/site-config.ts`.

## Original test page

The initial untracked file contained only `ORUWA` and `Corporate website test page.` It was intentionally replaced after the user confirmed it was a test index; no product or customer work was present.
